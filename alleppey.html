Alleppey

Alleppey (Alappuzha) sits on a network of canals, lagoons and Vembanad Lake and is the best-known starting point for houseboat trips.

Top things to do

  • Take an overnight or day houseboat cruise
  • Ride a shikara or village canoe through narrow canals
  • Visit Alleppey Beach
  • Watch the Nehru Trophy Boat Race, held in August

Best time to visit

November to February for the calmest, driest weather.

Getting there

Kochi airport is about 80 km away (roughly 2 hours by road). Alleppey also has a railway station.
