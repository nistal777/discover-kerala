Destinations

Choose a beach, backwater or hill station to start planning.

Munnar

Hill station

A hill station in the Western Ghats of Idukki district, Munnar is known for rolling tea plantations, cool air and misty viewpoints.

Alleppey

Backwaters

Alleppey (Alappuzha) sits on a network of canals, lagoons and Vembanad Lake and is the best-known starting point for houseboat trips.

Kovalam

Beach

Kovalam is a beach destination near Thiruvananthapuram with sheltered crescent-shaped bays, palm trees and a lighthouse.

Athirappilly

Waterfall

Athirappilly Falls drops from the Chalakudy River in Thrissur district, surrounded by rainforest. Nearby Vazhachal Falls adds a second stop.
