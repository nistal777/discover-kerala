Munnar

A hill station in the Western Ghats of Idukki district, Munnar is known for rolling tea plantations, cool air and misty viewpoints.

Top things to do

  • Walk through tea plantations and visit a tea museum
  • See Nilgiri tahr at Eravikulam National Park
  • Relax by Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point
  • Trek the hills around the town

Best time to visit

September to May, with cooler weather from October to March.

Getting there

Kochi is the nearest major airport, about 110 km away (roughly 4 hours by road).
