Kovalam

Kovalam is a beach destination near Thiruvananthapuram with sheltered crescent-shaped bays, palm trees and a lighthouse.

Top things to do

  • Swim and sunbathe at Lighthouse Beach and Hawa Beach
  • Climb the lighthouse for coastal views
  • Try an Ayurvedic massage
  • Eat fresh seafood at beachfront cafes

Best time to visit

September to March, when the sea is calmer.

Getting there

Thiruvananthapuram airport is about 15 km away (roughly 30 to 40 minutes by road).
