About Kerala

A narrow strip of land between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats in south-west India, nicknamed "God's Own Country".

Quick facts

  • Capital: Thiruvananthapuram
  • Main language: Malayalam (English is widely understood in tourist areas)
  • Currency: Indian rupee (INR)
  • Climate: Tropical, with the main monsoon from June to September
  • Best time to visit: October to March for most travellers

Culture and festivals

Kerala is known for Kathakali dance, Kalaripayattu martial arts, temple festivals and Onam, the harvest festival celebrated in August or September with flower carpets, boat races and a large feast.

Food

Coconut, rice, seafood and spices shape Kerala cooking. Try appam with stew, fish curry, and the banana-leaf meal called sadya.

Travel tips

  • Check visa and entry rules for your passport on the official Government of India website before booking.
  • Pack light cotton clothes and a rain jacket, and dress modestly at temples.
  • Book houseboats and hill-station stays ahead during peak season, December to January.
