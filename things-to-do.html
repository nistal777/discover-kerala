Things to Do in Kerala

Explore the beauty, culture and adventures of God's Own Country.

Trekking in Munnar

Enjoy beautiful mountain views and explore the green hills of Munnar.

Houseboat Cruise

Relax on a traditional houseboat and enjoy the peaceful Kerala backwaters.

Ayurveda Spa Retreat

Experience traditional Ayurvedic treatments and enjoy a relaxing break.

Wildlife Safari

Discover Kerala's forests and look for elephants, birds and other wildlife.

Kathakali Cultural Show

Watch Kerala's famous traditional dance with colourful costumes and makeup.

Spice Plantation Tour

Explore spice gardens and learn about the spices grown in Kerala.
